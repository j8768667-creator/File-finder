Harmony walks a per-genre Markov transition matrix over scale degrees, then is voiced by nearest-neighbour voice leading (common tones held, other voices moved by step). Melodies are built from a motif that is transposed, inverted, displaced and ornamented across the form, with leap-then-step recovery, passing/neighbour tones and appoggiaturas. Exports a Type 1 multitrack MIDI file with velocities.
